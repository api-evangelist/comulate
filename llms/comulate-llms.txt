# Comulate

> Comulate is an accounting automation and revenue intelligence platform for large insurance brokers. It captures carrier statement data across formats, reconciles direct-bill and payables transactions to policies, and surfaces commission forecasting and variance tracking. No public developer API or documentation is published as of 2026-07-18; the platform is delivered as a hosted application at app.comulate.com with native connectors to broker systems.

## Products
- [Direct Bill Automation](https://comulate.com): capture carrier statement data (spreadsheets, PDFs, email) and auto-reconcile transactions to policies
- [Carrier Payables Automation](https://comulate.com): streamline payables, flag discrepancies, manage disbursement timing
- [Revenue Intelligence](https://comulate.com): commission forecasting, variance tracking, missing-commission detection
